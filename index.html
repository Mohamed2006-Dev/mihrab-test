How to join

Use the same Google account for the group, the opt-in page, and Google Play.

  1. Join the tester group

    Open Google Groups and select “Join group.”

    Join the tester group

  2. Opt in to the test

    Open the web opt-in page and select “Become a tester.”

    Opt in to the test

  3. Install Mihrab on Google Play

    Once you’ve opted in, open the store listing and install the app.

    Install Mihrab on Google Play

After installing, try setting your city and reminders or opening a Quran reading assignment. You can leave feedback through the test listing on Google Play.
